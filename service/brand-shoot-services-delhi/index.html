What are brand shoot services?

Brand shoot services are planned photo and video shoots that show your brand, your people and your products in one clear style. A brand shoot in Delhi usually covers the idea, the shot list, the shoot day, editing and final files for your website, ads, social media and online stores.

Most brands do not need more photos. They need the right photos. One good brand shoot can fill your website, your Instagram, your Amazon listings and your ads for months.

What makes the best brand shoot services in Delhi?

We cannot tell you who is best for you. But here is what to check before you hire anyone:

  1. They plan before they shoot. Ask to see a moodboard and a shot list.
  2. They ask about your customers. A good shoot starts with your buyer, not the camera.
  3. They show real work for brands like yours. Look at their portfolio, not just their claims.
  4. Their pricing is clear. You should know what is included and what costs extra.
  5. They know where the photos will be used. A website banner and an Amazon listing need different pictures.
  6. They tell you who owns the files. Get this in writing.
  7. They can help after the shoot. Photos are only useful if they get posted, run in ads and show up on your website.

That last point is where we are different. Viral Ads Media also runs social media, SEO, websites and ads. So we shoot with the finished use already in mind. Skipping steps is how you end up with photos instead of a brand, and we do not skip steps.

Shooting in Delhi: studio, location, permits and weather

Studio or location? A studio gives you control over light and is best for products. A location gives you real surroundings and is best for people, teams and lifestyle shots. We can also shoot at your office, shop or factory.

Permits. Public gardens and cafés are easy for most shoots. Government-protected monuments, like many in Delhi, need advance permission for commercial photography. We check this before we quote, because it can change your date and your cost.

Season and weather.

  • Winter (Nov to Jan): smog can make outdoor photos look flat and grey. We plan indoor shoots or clear morning slots.
  • Summer (Apr to Jun): heat is hard on food, makeup and models. We shoot early or indoors.
  • Monsoon (Jul to Sep): rain can cancel outdoor plans, so we keep a backup date.

How to get ready for your brand shoot

  1. Write down what you want the photos to do (more sales, more calls, better trust).
  2. Collect 5 to 10 pictures whose style you like.
  3. Make a list of every product, person and place to shoot.
  4. Clean, iron and test your products before the day.
  5. Tell us where each photo will be used.
  6. Keep your logo and brand colours handy.
  7. Pick one person on your side who can approve things fast.

Brand shoot FAQs

Everything you need to know about our workflow, deliverables, and service execution.

What is a brand shoot?

A brand shoot is a planned photo and video session that shows your brand's look, people and products. The images are used on your website, social media, ads and online stores.

What is the difference between a brand shoot and a product shoot?

A brand shoot shows who you are, with people, places and products together. A product shoot focuses only on the item you sell and its details. Many brands do both in the same session.

How long does a brand shoot take?

Most shoots take a few hours to one full day. Planning comes first, and editing comes after. We share the delivery time in your quote.

Do you shoot for Amazon and Flipkart?

Yes. We shoot ecommerce photography in Delhi for online stores. We follow each store's rules on background, size and image count.

Do you provide models, stylists and makeup artists?

Yes, when your shoot needs them. We add them to your quote so you can see the cost.

Can you shoot at our office, shop or factory?

Yes. We can shoot at your place, at a studio, or at a Delhi location.
