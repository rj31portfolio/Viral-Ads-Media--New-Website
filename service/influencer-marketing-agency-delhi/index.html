What does an influencer marketing agency in Delhi do?

An influencer marketing agency connects your brand with social media creators and runs the whole campaign for you. That means finding creators, checking them, handling brand collaboration deals, approving content, and measuring results. You get one team instead of fifty chat threads.

In simple words, we do the work that eats your week:

  • Find creators whose followers match your buyers
  • Check that their followers and engagement are real
  • Talk to the creators and agree on price and rights
  • Guide the content so it sounds like them, not like an ad
  • Track sales, clicks and cost, then fix what is not working

How does an influencer campaign work with us?

A good campaign follows six steps. We do not skip any of them.

  1. Goal call. We ask what you want: sales, app installs, store visits or awareness. We also ask who buys from you.
  2. Creator discovery. We build a shortlist and check each profile.
  3. Outreach and deal. We contact the creators, agree on price, content type and usage rights, and sign a simple contract.
  4. Content guide and approval. We share a short brief. The creator keeps their own style. You approve before it goes live.
  5. Launch and boost. Posts go live with the right disclosure. We can boost the best ones as partnership ads.
  6. Report and improve. You get a report. We keep what worked and drop what did not.

How do you choose the best influencer marketing agency in Delhi?

Look for proof, not promises. Whether you pick us or someone else, ask these seven questions:

  1. Can you show real results from brands like mine?
  2. How do you check for fake followers?
  3. Who will actually run my campaign?
  4. What exactly does your fee cover?
  5. Do I own the content, and for how long?
  6. How will you report results, and how often?
  7. How do you handle ASCI disclosure rules?

Red flags: guaranteed viral results, no price breakdown, no reporting, and a creator list that looks like a bought database.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our workflow, deliverables, and service execution.

What does an influencer marketing agency in Delhi do?

It finds and vets creators, plans the campaign, handles contracts and content approvals, and reports results. Viral Ads Media also boosts the best creator posts as ads and tracks sales so you know what worked.

What is the best influencer marketing company in Delhi for small businesses?

The best one is the one that shows real results, explains its fees and reports clearly. For small budgets, look for agencies that work with nano and micro creators. We do, and we start small.

How long does it take to see results?

Views and engagement show within days. Sales usually need two to four weeks of data. A few good campaigns tell you more than one.

What is influencer whitelisting?

Whitelisting means running ads through a creator's account with their permission. People see the ad from someone they trust. It often builds more trust than a brand-handle ad.

Do you work with brands across Delhi NCR?

Yes. We work with brands in Delhi, Noida and Gurugram, and in Rohini and nearby areas. Our office is in Budh Vihar, Delhi.

Can influencer marketing increase sales, or only awareness?

It can do both. Use promo codes, affiliate links and partnership ads to push sales. Pure awareness posts work best for launches.
