What is performance marketing?

Performance marketing is paid advertising where you pay for, and measure, real results like leads, sales or app installs. You judge it by numbers such as cost per lead (CPL), cost per sale (CPA) and return on ad spend (ROAS), not by views or reach.

In simple terms: regular marketing tells people you exist. Performance marketing tracks who acted because of your ad.

ComparisonRegular digital marketingPerformance marketing
Main goalAwareness and brandLeads and sales
Judged byReach, likes, viewsCPL, CPA, ROAS, revenue
FeedbackSlowFast, often within days
TrackingLightDetailed, ad to sale

Is it right for you? It works best when you have a clear offer, a working website or landing page, and a way to follow up with leads. It does not work well if you can't track results, or if you expect big numbers from a very small budget in week one. We'll tell you honestly if it's not the right time.

What works on Instagram in 2026?

Instagram now rewards Reels that people watch to the end and send to friends. Original videos beat reposts, and the first three seconds decide most of the result. Here is what we build our Reels plans around.

  • Watch time comes first. Instagram's head, Adam Mosseri, has named watch time, sends per reach and likes per reach as the signals that matter most. A Reel people finish and share beats one that only collects hearts.
  • Make it yours. Instagram demotes videos that carry another app's watermark. Accounts that repost heavily can be left out of recommendations.
  • Longer Reels can work. Reels up to three minutes now reach non-followers. Short is not the only way.
  • Test before you commit. Trial Reels let accounts with over 1,000 followers show a Reel to non-followers first.
  • Keep it steady. Instagram tends to reward accounts that post on a regular rhythm, so a plan you can keep beats a burst you can't.
